05 — Kill Criteria

When to
Pull the Plug.

Grooming is a replenishment category. If the customer doesn't come back, no marketing spend fixes it.

Primary Kill Signal — Month 6
18%
RPR

If RPR falls below 18% at Month 6 — shut it down. No pivots. The product failed to earn its place in his routine.

Deodorant lasts 3–4 weeks. Body wash, 3–5. If the product works, reorders happen within 6–8 weeks. Below 18% RPR means fewer than 1 in 5 first buyers came back. That's not a marketing problem — it's a PMF failure.

↗ Success Zone
RPR above 28% with organic UGC appearing unprompted + campus trials converting to repeat orders — double the marketing budget immediately.
